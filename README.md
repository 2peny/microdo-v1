# Microdo

Microdo is a focused learning app that helps people turn large amounts of study material into smaller, structured learning steps.

Instead of keeping notes, documents, courses, and study material scattered across different places, Microdo brings them into one focused space where users can work through content in a clearer and more manageable way.

## What Microdo is for

Microdo is built for people who want to:

- Study from their own learning material
- Break large topics into smaller, easier sections
- Organize courses or study modules
- Upload learning documents and work from them
- Review content without feeling overwhelmed
- Keep their learning journey structured and easy to follow

## Who it is for

Microdo is especially useful for:

- Students
- Self-directed learners
- People studying professional or technical material
- Anyone working through PDFs, Word documents, notes, or structured course content

## How it works

Users can bring learning material into Microdo, organise it into study modules, and move through the material in smaller, focused steps.

The app is designed around the idea that learning becomes easier when large amounts of information are broken down into manageable sections.

## Key Features

- **Structured learning modules**  
  Organise learning content into clear sections and topics.

- **Document uploads**  
  Bring in learning material from PDF and Word documents.

- **Study guidance**  
  Help users understand where to start and what to work on next.

- **Focused learning flow**  
  Work through content one section at a time instead of navigating large amounts of material at once.

- **Course organisation**  
  Keep different subjects, courses, and study material separated and easy to navigate.

- **Learning review**  
  Revisit and inspect study content when more detail or revision is needed.

- **Simple visual structure**  
  Make complex learning material easier to understand and navigate.

## The idea behind Microdo

Microdo is based on a simple principle:

**Large learning goals become easier when they are broken into smaller, manageable actions.**

The goal is not to replace the learning material itself, but to make it easier to organise, understand, and work through.
